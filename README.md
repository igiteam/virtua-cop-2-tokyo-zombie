## Tokyo Zombie — Kickstarter

Welcome to **Tokyo Zombie**, an original arcade rail shooter set in a vibrant, alternate 1990s Tokyo on the brink of collapse. Neon-lit streets and bustling neighborhoods are now overrun by relentless waves of the undead. You play as a rookie detective partnered with a seasoned veteran, sent deep into Tokyo's most iconic districts — from the crowded alleys of Shibuya to the claustrophobic tunnels of the Tokyo Metro. Contain the outbreak, protect survivors, and uncover the dark mystery behind the zombie invasion.

Unlike other zombie games that dwell in horror and gloom, Tokyo Zombie embraces the energy and color of 90s Tokyo, blending nostalgic sights and sounds with intense, fast-paced arcade action.

**Virtua Cop + Zombies = Tokyo Zombie**

### Engine & Tech

The game uses an open-source version of **idTech3** — the engine behind Quake 3, MOHAA, COD, and many successors. Built with VS Code, compiled for Linux Ubuntu. No software copyright fees, so resellers can focus on selling the arcade game itself.

### Story & Setting

The idea came from a vivid image: an office in a Tokyo skyscraper, daylight, two police officers arrive to interview a witness — then screams, a zombie throws a man out the window, and you draw your gun. Levels include the subway, Tokyo streets, shopping centres. The whole story happens during daytime, making it more young-adult friendly.

**Characters:** Akio, a young cop living in Tokyo; his partner Asaka, a female detective. They're working a case together during the outbreak, unaware how deep their investigation will go.

**Colours:** Vibrant daylight palette inspired by Virtua Cop, Crisis Zone, Confidential Mission, and House of the Dead.

**Music:** Rocky, fast-paced metal tunes with recognisable guitar licks, blending zombie sounds, gun sounds, and lightgun recoil noise.

### Why It Matters

The arcade industry is still a hidden monetised industry dominated by big players like SEGA and Namco. To penetrate the market, we need great games that let independent companies access tools and communities beyond these barriers, and share open-source software.

**At the end of this project, with your help, we would have:**
- [x] Tokyo Zombie Arcade Cabinet with recoil 'Armory Vault' lightgun pack
- [x] Tokyo Zombie Arcade Game running on Linux Ubuntu
- [x] Tokyo Zombie idTech3 open-source rail-shooter source code for the community (GitHub, Forgejo, Codeberg)
- [x] Possible new entrances of other arcade games
- [x] Fans can enjoy a cool new zombie-themed vibrant shooter
- [x] Arcade collectors can contribute to help develop new titles for this niche market
